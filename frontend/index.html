    10 Password Security Rules
    1. Use unique passwords for different accounts.
    2. Prefer longer passwords or randomly generated passphrases.
    3. Avoid predictable personal information.
    4. Avoid common passwords.
    5. Never reuse passwords for important accounts.
    6. Use a password manager.
    7. Enable MFA where available.
    8. Never share passwords.
    9. Be cautious of phishing.
    10. Change passwords when compromise is suspected or confirmed.
